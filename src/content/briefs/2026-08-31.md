- [AJGA Debuts in South Dakota This Week as Junior at Red Rock Qualifier Goes Wednesday in Rapid City](https://www.ajga.org/tournaments/2026138) — AJGA
  The AJGA's new three-year Rapid City partnership tips off with a Wednesday qualifier at the Golf Club at Red Rock, followed by a Junior-Am and practice round Thursday and 54 holes of stroke play Sept. 4-6.   The Women's Met Amateur champion birdied four of her last five on the front nine Sunday for a 66, the best round of the week at the U.S. Women's Mid-Amateur, after opening with a 7-over 77. Only seven sub-par rounds were posted across two days; the cut fell at 14-over 154, tying the championship record, and McEvoy meets Taya Battistella at 1:10 p.m. in today's Round of 64.

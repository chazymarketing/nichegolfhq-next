- [Kuchar Taps Sohan Patel as Captain's Pick; Miles Russell Headlines First 11 for Junior Presidents Cup at Medinah](https://www.ajga.org/tournaments/2026142/junior-presidents-cup-announces-first-11-u-s-team-players-and-final-international-team-player-for-2026-competition) — AJGA

- [Ina Kim-Schaad Defends at Montclair as 132-Player U.S. Women's Mid-Amateur Field Tees Off Saturday](https://www.nichegolfhq.com/midamgolfhq/usga-womens-mid-am) — nichegolfHQ
  The championship runs Aug. 29-Sept. 3 over Montclair Golf Club's No. 2 and No. 3 nines in West Orange, N.J., with 36 holes of stroke play cutting to a 64-player match-play bracket. Kim-Schaad returns after outlasting Hanley Long in 23 holes at Monterey Peninsula last year, the longest final in event history.
  Tags: mid-am

- [Mike McCoy Defends U.S. Senior Amateur as 156 Head to Baltimore CC East Course](https://www.nichegolfhq.com/seniorgolfhq/usga-mens-senior-amateur) — nichegolfHQ
  The 71st U.S. Senior Amateur opens Saturday at Baltimore Country Club, the club's first USGA championship since the 1988 U.S. Women's Open, with 36 holes of stroke play preceding match play and a Sept. 3 final. McCoy, who in 2025 became the first player to win both a U.S. Mid-Amateur and a U.S. Senior Amateur, is exempt and back to defend.

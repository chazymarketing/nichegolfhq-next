- [Van Dam Names 12-Player European Team for PING Junior Solheim Cup at Bernardus](https://ladieseuropeantour.com/blog/van-dam-announces-european-team-for-2026-ping-junior-solheim-cup/) — Ladies European Tour

- [Rank Wins Fourth Canadian Mid-Amateur Title; Collins Fires Opening 65 to Lead Valentine Invitational](https://www.nichegolfhq.com/midamgolfhq/canadian-mens-mid-amateur/2026-canadian-mens-mid-amateur-final-recap) — nichegolfHQ

The victory earns Rank an exemption into the U.S. Mid-Amateur at Sand Valley. Meanwhile at Hermitage Country Club, Will Collins of Charlottesville fired a 7-under 65 on the Sabot Course to grab a four-shot lead after Round 1 of the 84th Valentine Invitational, with Nicolas Brown, Blanton Farmer, and Winburne Hughes sharing second at 3-under 69. - [U.S. Senior Amateur Field of 156 Heads to Baltimore CC With McCoy Defending, Aug. 29-Sept. 3](https://championships.usga.org/ussenioramateur/2026/articles/fast-facts-2026-us-senior-amateur.html) — USGA
  The 71st U.S. Senior Amateur opens Aug. 29 on Baltimore Country Club's par-70 East Course, with two stroke-play rounds cutting to a 64-man match-play bracket and the final on Sept. 3. Mike McCoy returns as defending champion a year after becoming the first player in USGA history to win both the U.S. Mid-Amateur and U.S. Senior Amateur.

- [Ina Kim-Schaad Defends at Montclair as 132-Player U.S. Women's Mid-Amateur Field Opens Saturday](https://www.nichegolfhq.com/midamgolfhq/usga-womens-mid-am/2026-us-womens-mid-amateur-preview) — nichegolfHQ
  The 39th U.S. Women's Mid-Amateur begins Aug. 29 at Montclair Golf Club in West Orange, N.J., with two rounds of stroke play before the low 64 advance to match play. Two-time champion Kim-Schaad returns after outlasting Hanley Long in 23 holes last year, the longest final in championship history, with the winner earning an exemption into the 2027 U.S. Women's Open.

- [156 Players Set for 71st U.S. Senior Amateur at Baltimore CC, Where McCoy Chases History Again](https://championships.usga.org/ussenioramateur/2026/articles/fast-facts-2026-us-senior-amateur.html) — USGA
  The championship opens Saturday on Baltimore Country Club's par-70 East Course, with 36 holes of stroke play trimming the field to 64 for match play and an 18-hole final Sept. 3. Defending champion Mike McCoy, the only player in USGA history to win both a U.S. Mid-Amateur and U.S. Senior Amateur, headlines a field playing a course that has hosted a U.S. Open, PGA Championship, U.S. Amateur and Walker Cup.
